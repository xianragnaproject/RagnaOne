# Classic 2-1 second jobs only

Supported packs:

- Assassin (Thief → Assassin)
- Knight (Swordman → Knight)
- Wizard (Magician → Wizard)
- Hunter (Archer → Hunter)
- Priest (Acolyte → Priest)
- Blacksmith (Merchant → Blacksmith)

Not included: 2-2 jobs (Crusader, Sage, Monk, Alchemist, Rogue, Bard, Dancer).

Job Master: Prontera `150,180`. Second-job menu choice is `r0` for every 2-1 path.

## Clean packs (AssassinClean progression)

Early-game packs mirroring AssassinClean:

- Field Scout on `prt_fild08` while Novice
- Job Master → first job
- Buy starter weapon + Cotton Shirt
- Save Payon, switch shops to Payon, hunt `pay_fild08`

Packs: `assassin_clean`, `knight_clean`, `wizard_clean`, `hunter_clean`, `priest_clean`, `blacksmith_clean`

Profiles: `{Class}Clean{N}` (e.g. `KnightClean1`)
